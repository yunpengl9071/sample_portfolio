Rare Disease Digital Endpoint

Project Duration: 30 months
Therapeutic Area: Rare Disease
Impact: Remote monitoring capability for pediatric trials

Challenge: Pediatric rare disease trials faced unique challenges with patient recruitment and retention due to geographic constraints and frequent clinic visits. Traditional clinical endpoints were often inadequate for capturing disease progression in rare pediatric conditions.

Solution: Our team developed innovative digital biomarkers using wearable technology, mobile health applications, and AI-powered analysis to enable remote monitoring of pediatric patients with rare diseases.

Implementation: We created a comprehensive digital health platform that captured movement patterns, sleep quality, activity levels, and patient-reported outcomes through child-friendly interfaces. Advanced analytics processed this continuous data stream to detect meaningful changes in disease status.

Results: The digital endpoint platform enabled successful completion of pivotal pediatric rare disease trials with improved patient engagement and reduced burden on families.

Key Achievements:

  • 95% patient retention rate
  • 80% reduction in clinic visits
  • Real-time safety monitoring capabilities
  • FDA qualification of digital endpoint
  • Family satisfaction score: 9.2/10
  • Expanded to 3 additional rare disease programs

Regulatory Recognition: The digital biomarker approach received FDA qualification and has been incorporated into regulatory guidance for rare disease drug development, setting new standards for the industry.
