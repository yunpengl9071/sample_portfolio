Regulatory Science Innovation

Project Duration: 42 months
Therapeutic Area: Cross-Portfolio
Impact: FDA guidance adoption

Challenge: Traditional drug development approaches were becoming increasingly inefficient, with regulatory agencies seeking more innovative, science-based methodologies that could accelerate approval timelines while maintaining safety standards.

Solution: Our team pioneered a model-informed drug development (MIDD) approach that integrated quantitative pharmacology, biomarker modeling, and clinical trial simulation to optimize drug development strategies.

Implementation: We developed a comprehensive MIDD platform that combined population pharmacokinetic modeling, pharmacodynamic analysis, and clinical trial simulation to inform dose selection, trial design, and regulatory strategy across multiple therapeutic areas.

Results: The MIDD methodology was successfully implemented across our development portfolio and gained recognition from regulatory authorities, ultimately being incorporated into FDA guidance documents.

Key Accomplishments:

  • FDA guidance document publication
  • EMA qualification opinion received
  • Implemented across 15+ development programs
  • 30% reduction in late-stage failures
  • 25% faster regulatory review times
  • 12 regulatory submissions utilizing MIDD

Industry Impact: Our MIDD approach has set new industry standards and has been adopted by multiple pharmaceutical companies, demonstrating the transformative potential of data-driven regulatory science.
